# US Wellness editorial design

Warm paper (#fbfaf5), forest ink (#173b33), deep green (#123f38), pale olive (#eef2e8), golden action accent (#f3c45b), fine rules (#d7dbd0). Self-hosted Forma DJR Display Bold headings and masthead, Forma DJR Text UI, Georgia article text. Desktop grid: 1200px max wrapper, 215px navigation, 780px article; hero splits headline and real product photograph. Mobile collapses to a single reading column with 20px gutters and a bottom product CTA.

The publication masthead, edition line, readable serif article, restrained comparison table and original product imagery form the visual identity. No decorative cards, invented ratings, background effects or generated replacement labels. Section spacing 40–56px; body copy 17–18px with 1.75–1.8 line height. Product feature uses pale olive, final CTA uses deep green with a gold button. Interactive controls have explicit focus, native FAQ details and a privacy dialog. Reduced-motion users receive no animation.

Browser QA at 1440x1000 and 390x844: no horizontal page overflow, all images/fonts loaded, four tracked product links, FAQs and privacy dialog working, no JavaScript errors. Desktop/mobile full-page captures visually inspected. Impeccable launcher unavailable, so no detector or separate reviewer ran; bounded direct visual inspection was used.
