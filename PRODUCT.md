# US Wellness Community

An editorial publication at USWellness.fit for visitors arriving from wellness ads. The page helps readers who disliked their first sea moss gel compare sourcing, ingredients, flavor and everyday use, then visit the official Healistix product page. The surface is a Read/Persuade hybrid: article credibility and an easy next step. Healistix is the featured product, not the masthead identity.

The page stores no visitor records and has no backend. It runs as Cloudflare Workers Static Assets. Product CTAs use US Wellness campaign parameters. Supplied source editorial guides the narrative; unsupported testing, testimonials and numeric health claims are not independently asserted.
