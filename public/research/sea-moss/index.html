The Sourcing Reality – Why Origin Matters

Volcanic Ocean Mineralization (St. Lucia): True sea moss must absorb raw minerals directly from ocean currents fed by volcanic rock beds. St. Lucia’s protected Caribbean waters yield sea moss naturally rich in potassium, iodine, calcium, and magnesium without industrial runoff.

Small-Batch Real-Fruit Preparation: Overseas bulk gels sit in shipping containers for months, relying on heavy chemical preservatives and synthetic flavoring. Sourcing raw, wildcrafted St. Lucia ocean moss and preparing it in small batches with real fruit purees (like Mango) preserves both maximum mineral potency and a fresh, smooth taste.
